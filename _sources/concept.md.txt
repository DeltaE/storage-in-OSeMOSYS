# Concept

<p class="page-intro">
A battery or a reservoir is only useful because of what happened before: what it can give
tonight depends on how much it stored this afternoon. Energy planning models save time by
simulating a few typical days instead of the whole year, and in doing so they can lose that link
between hours and days. This section explains the problem in plain terms and introduces the
four ways of modelling storage that the study compares.
</p>

## The four formulations at a glance

Each card opens the full description with its analogy.

<div class="section-cards section-cards--four">
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#niet-direct-link-stacked-diary">
    <span class="section-card__step">Niet</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Stacked diary"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">Representative slices in a row, each weighted by the days it stands for. Small, but can overstate storage.</span>
  </a>
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#welsch-fixed-pattern-photocopied-day">
    <span class="section-card__step">Welsch</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Photocopied day"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">Each day type repeated as a block. Compact, but the day order is lost.</span>
  </a>
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#kotzur-day-boundary-verified-daily-ledger">
    <span class="section-card__step">Kotzur</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Daily ledger"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">Days linked in calendar order; limits checked only at day boundaries.</span>
  </a>
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#novo-time-step-verified-hourly-ledger">
    <span class="section-card__step">Novo</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Hourly ledger"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">Every time block of the year in calendar order, limits checked at each one. Most faithful, largest model.</span>
  </a>
</div>
