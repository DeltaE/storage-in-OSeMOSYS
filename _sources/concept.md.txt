# Concept

<p class="page-intro">
A battery or a reservoir is only useful because of what happened before: what it can give
tonight depends on how much it stored this afternoon. Energy planning models save time by
simulating a few typical days instead of the whole year, and in doing so they can lose that link
between hours and days. This section explains the problem in plain terms and introduces the
four ways of modelling storage that the study compares.
</p>

## The four formulations at a glance

Each card opens the full description with its analogy.

<div class="section-cards section-cards--four">
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#niet-direct-link-continuous-diary">
    <span class="section-card__step">Niet</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Continuous diary"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">Each day picks up exactly where the last one ended. Most faithful, most data.</span>
  </a>
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#welsch-fixed-pattern-photocopied-day">
    <span class="section-card__step">Welsch</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Photocopied day"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">One typical day repeated. Fast and simple, but every day looks the same.</span>
  </a>
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#kotzur-unverified-cluster-alternating-diaries">
    <span class="section-card__step">Kotzur</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Alternating diaries"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">Several typical days capture variety, but days are not linked to each other.</span>
  </a>
  <a class="section-card section-card--mini" href="notes/about_storage_algorithms.html#novo-hourly-verified-linked-journaling">
    <span class="section-card__step">Novo</span>
    <span class="section-card__tag">"Linked journaling"</span>
    <span class="section-card__text">Typical days, with each one starting where the previous one ended. The middle ground.</span>
  </a>
</div>
